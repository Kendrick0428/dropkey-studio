Pricing questions

Good to know

Will Dropkey Studio stay free?

It's free during early access. We'll announce paid editions before they launch. [Decide what free users keep after launch, e.g. a free tier with today's features.]

What happens to my projects when paid editions launch?

Your saved project files and exported audio stay yours. Keep a project file of anything you care about as a backup.

What will the refund policy be?

Full refund within 14 days if you haven't activated your license. If you've activated it and Dropkey won't run on your device, contact support within 14 days. If we can't fix it, we'll refund you. Email kendrick78@yahoo.com with your order number.

What happens when my 12 months of updates end?

Dropkey keeps working, and you keep the latest version you received. Renew whenever you want newer versions for another 12 months: $29 for Starter, $49 for Producer or $99 for Studio.
