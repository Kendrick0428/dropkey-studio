A workflow that follows your idea

  1. 1Program a drum loop on the step grid
  2. 2Add a bassline and chords in the piano roll
  3. 3Arrange patterns into a song, bar by bar
  4. 4Mix it and export an MP3 or WAV to share

Sounds built in, or bring your own

20 drum sounds in 5 ready-made kits, and 18 instruments, from 808s and acid bass to electric piano, strings, choir and supersaw. Import your own WAV or MP3 samples and play them on the drum grid or piano roll.

Works on what you have

Runs in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. No install and no account. Headphones help you hear the low end. Record vocals straight from your mic. MIDI keyboard support is planned for a later version.

Your work stays yours

Projects save automatically in your browser. Save a project file to back up your work or move it to another device, and export MP3 or WAV files whenever you're ready.

Inside Dropkey Studio

The five tools you'll use on every beat

Piano roll

Write basslines, chords and melodies note by note. Tap to add a note, drag to make it longer, and hear every change instantly.

  • Scale lock shows only notes that fit your key
  • See your bass notes while you write chords
  • Works with a mouse, a finger or the keyboard

Instruments

18 built-in instruments and 20 drum sounds, plus any sample you import. Each one sounds good the moment you pick it.

  • Bass: 808 (regular, long and distorted), sub, Reese, acid and square
  • Keys, pads and leads: electric piano, organ, marimba, bell, pluck, strings, choir, brass, supersaw and more
  • Drum kits: Classic, Trap, Lo-fi, House and Percussion
  • Your own samples: drum hits, loops and vocal chops, plus a chopper to slice them
  • Record vocals over your beat

Mixer

Every sound gets volume, pan, a 3-band EQ, compression, drive, reverb and a tempo-synced delay. The master channel adds a filter, warmth and gentle compression so everything sits together.

  • Mute or solo any sound to hear what it adds
  • Tune and shorten drums with pitch and decay
  • Automate filter sweeps, fades and pans for build-ups and drops

Song arranger

Build up to 16 patterns, A to P, each up to 4 bars long, then place them in order to turn a loop into a full song with an intro, verses and hooks.

  • Up to 64 blocks per song, up to 4 bars each
  • Up to 16 drum rows per pattern
  • See your song's length and running time as you build
  • Copy a pattern to start a variation in one tap

Questions

Before you start

Still unsure? Email kendrick78@yahoo.com.

Is it really free?

Yes. Dropkey Studio is free during early access, with no account and no payment details. Paid editions with more features are planned, and we'll announce prices before anything changes. Your saved project files and exports stay yours either way.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Dropkey Studio runs in your web browser. It works on a computer, tablet or phone, though a computer with headphones is the easiest way to work.

Where are my projects saved?

Automatically, in the browser you're using. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so use "Save project file" to keep a backup or move a project to another device.

Can I release music I make with Dropkey?

Yes. Every built-in sound is generated by Dropkey itself, so beats made with them are yours to share and release. If you import samples, make sure you have the rights to use them.

Can I use my own sounds or record vocals?

Yes. Import your own samples (WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC or M4A), chop loops into slices, and play them on the drum grid or piano roll. You can also record vocals over your beat with a one-bar count-in; your browser asks for microphone access the first time. MIDI keyboard support is planned for a later version.

Do I need a MIDI keyboard or other gear?

No. You can make complete beats with a mouse, trackpad or finger. Headphones help you hear the bass.
