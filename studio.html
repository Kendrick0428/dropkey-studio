Drums · A

Tap a square to add a hit; tap again to remove it. Turn on Accent to place louder hits. Choose any sound for a row from its menu, and tap ▶ to hear it. M mutes a row, S plays it alone. Space plays and stops. Load whole kits or your own samples on the Sounds tab.

Melody · A

Tap to add a note, drag right while adding to make it longer, and tap a note to remove it. Tap a key on the left to hear it. Dashed outlines show the other instrument's notes. Keyboard: arrows move, Enter adds or removes, [ and ] change length.

Song

Pick a pattern above, then tap blocks to place it in order. Each block plays its whole pattern, so a 2-bar pattern fills 2 bars; the small number is the bar where the block starts. Tap a block again to clear it. An empty block in the middle is one bar of silence.

Levels: volume, pan, reverb, delay and tone for each sound, plus pitch and decay for drums. Effects: a 3-band EQ, compression and drive on each sound, and the master delay's timing. Mix settings apply to every pattern; use the Automate tab to change them over time.

Automation · A

Automation changes a control over time inside this pattern, like a filter sweep before the drop or a fade at the end. Drag across a lane to draw. The dashed line is the control's normal Mix setting, used wherever nothing is drawn; a drawn value holds until the next one. Place the pattern in your song to hear it there. It's included when you export.

Microphone is off

Wear headphones so the beat doesn't leak into the mic. You'll hear a one-bar count-in, then recording starts on the downbeat. Each take is saved in Your samples below.

Drum kits

A kit swaps the sound on every drum row. Your beats stay the same. You can still pick any sound for a single row on the Drums tab.
