    Words to know

    Producer terms in plain English

    BPM
    Beats per minute: how fast the song is. Hip-hop often sits around 80 to 100, house around 120 to 128.
    Bar
    A short repeating unit of time, usually four beats. Most loops are 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars long.
    Step sequencer
    A grid where each square is a moment in the bar. Turn squares on to play a sound there.
    Piano roll
    A grid with piano keys down the side. Draw notes to write melodies, chords and basslines.
    Velocity
    How hard a note is hit. Varying it makes drums and keys sound less robotic.
    Swing
    Delaying every other step slightly so the groove bounces instead of marching.
    808
    A deep, booming bass drum sound often tuned to play bass notes, common in trap and hip-hop.
    Sidechain
    Making one sound duck when another plays, like bass dipping each time the kick hits.
    Stems
    Separate audio files for each part of a song (drums, bass, vocals) so others can remix or mix it.
